# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in issues, reviews, documentation and project-associated
communications should be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss the
technical issue; assume neither identity nor experience from a person's question.
Give actionable feedback and accept corrections without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
publication of private information and repeated disruption are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include the relevant
links and a concise account; do not post personal information publicly. The
maintainer acknowledges reports, reviews the available evidence and limits sharing
to what is needed to handle the report. No absolute confidentiality is promised.

Enforcement can include a private correction, warning, removal of content,
temporary participation limits or exclusion, proportionate to severity and
repetition. The affected person can privately request reconsideration and provide
new evidence. The project currently has one maintainer and no independent
moderator; a complaint involving the maintainer cannot receive independent
internal adjudication. Platform abuse reporting remains available.
